## Usage4Claude Codex implementation reference

CCMB's direct Codex usage architecture was derived from
[Usage4Claude](https://github.com/f-is-h/Usage4Claude): OAuth/credential
renewal followed by an authenticated `wham/usage` request, with privacy-safe
diagnostics. CCMB has its own AppKit integration, storage, parsing, UI, and
diagnostic implementation.
